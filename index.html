13 Assyrian Fathers in Georgia

6th Century Monastic Founders • Eastern Georgia

13 Monasteries
Mtskheta-Mtianeti
Ioane of Zedazeni
იოანე ზედაზნელი • Zedazeni Monastery
Leader of the mission from Mesopotamia in the mid-6th century. Chose Zedazeni mountain north of Mtskheta. Lived in ascetic solitude with bears as companions.
Kakheti (Gareja Desert)
Davit of Gareja
დავით გარეჯელი • David Gareja Lavra
Initially dwelt on Mount Mtatsminda, then retreated south into the harsh desert of Gareja where he lived in caves and drank milk from wild deer.
Mtskheta-Mtianeti
Shio of Mgvime
შიო მღვიმელი • Shio-Mgvime Monastery
Lived in a remote limestone canyon near Mtskheta. Thousands gathered to form a monastery under his guidance; spent his final 15 years in a deep pit tomb.
Kakheti (Alazani Valley)
Abibos of Nekresi
აბიბოს ნეკრესელი • Nekresi Monastery
Bishop of Nekresi. Preached among mountain tribes and extinguished a Zoroastrian sacred fire altar to show it had no divine power. Crowned with martyrdom.
Kvemo Kartli
Anton of Martqopi
ანტონ მარტყოფელი • Martqopi Monastery
Brought an authentic image of Christ on a tile (Mandylion) to Georgia. Later climbed a tall stone tower to spend 15 years in unbroken prayer as a stylite.
Kakheti (Signagi)
Stephane of Khirsa
სტეფანე ხირსელი • Khirsa Monastery
Sent east into fertile Kakheti to preach Christ near the Khirsa river, where he established a major basilica.
Kakheti (Telavi)
Zenon of Ikhalto
ზენონ იყალთოელი • Ikhalto Monastery
Founded the Holy Transfiguration cloister near Telavi, which centuries later became Georgia's famous Academy of philosophy and science.
Kakheti (Akhmeta)
Ioseb of Alaverdi
იოსებ ალავერდელი • Alaverdi Cathedral
Settled in the wild plains of Alaverdi. Preached to locals, founding the hermitage that later grew into the monumental 11th-century Alaverdi Cathedral
